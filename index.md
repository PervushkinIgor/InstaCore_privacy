Privacy Policy for InstaCore

Effective Date: 26.10.2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the InstaCore mobile application (hereinafter referred to as the "App") collects, uses, and handles your information.

1. Information Collection and Processing
Photos and Images: The App allows you to upload images from your gallery or capture photos with your camera for cropping, resampling, and caption generation purposes.

Local Processing: All operations related to resolution adjustment, cropping, and watermark overlaying are performed locally on your device.

Third-Party AI Services: To generate text descriptions (Alt-Text) and hashtags, the App transmits the selected image via a secure HTTPS connection to a third-party artificial intelligence service — Google Gemini API.

2. Data Privacy and Model Training
When images are sent to the Google Gemini API, their processing is governed by Google's privacy policy and terms of service.

Since the App utilizes a free-tier API, transmitted data (including uploaded images) may be used by Google in accordance with their terms to improve services and train AI models. We recommend avoiding the upload of photographs containing sensitive personal data, documents, or private information.

The App developers do not store, collect, or transfer your photographs to any other servers or third parties.

3. Data Rights and Security
All generated texts and processed photographs are saved directly to your device or your public gallery.

You may uninstall the App at any time, which removes all temporary files associated with it.

4. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
